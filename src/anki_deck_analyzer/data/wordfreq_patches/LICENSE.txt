The wordfreq_patch_*.json files in this folder are derived from wordfreq

  Robyn Speer. wordfreq: access a database of word frequencies, in various
  natural languages. https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
  (word lists of wordfreq 3.1.1)

Changes made: for each language, the top entries of wordfreq's list were
checked against a Hunspell spelling dictionary. Each patch records, per
entry, a decision: kept, merged into another spelling, set aside as a name,
or dropped with a reason (foreign word, misspelling, abbreviation, junk).
The patches hold words and decisions only; wordfreq's frequencies are not
included, and applying a patch reads them from the user's own wordfreq
installation.
