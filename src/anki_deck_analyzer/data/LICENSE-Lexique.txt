lexique383_lemmas.json is derived from Lexique 3.83

  New, B., Pallier, C., Brysbaert, M., & Ferrand, L. (2004). Lexique 2: A new
  French lexical database. Behavior Research Methods, Instruments, &
  Computers, 36(3), 516-524.
  New, B., Pallier, C., Ferrand, L., & Matos, R. (2001). Une base de données
  lexicales du français contemporain sur internet: LEXIQUE. L'Année
  Psychologique, 101, 447-462.
  http://www.lexique.org

Changes made: only the lemme, ortho, freqfilms2 and freqlivres columns are
used. Per lemma, freqfilms2 and freqlivres are each summed over its forms,
and the lemmas are ranked by the mean of the two sums. The file holds each
lemma, in that order, with the spellings Lexique gives for it; the
frequencies themselves are not included.
